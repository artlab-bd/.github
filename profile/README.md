# ART Lab

**Innovation in Research & Technology**

Advanced Research Thoughts Lab Ltd. (ART Lab) is a Bangladesh-based research, evaluation and data consultancy. We turn credible evidence into practical decisions.

## What we work on

- Research design and implementation
- Monitoring, evaluation and learning
- Data collection, analysis and visualization
- Digital tools and technology solutions
- Capacity development and knowledge products

## Open work

This organization shares selected tools, templates and open resources developed by ART Lab. Project documentation and contribution guidance will be published with each repository.
